Master’s Thesis

A footwear and bag collection that starts from the accessory of the 1920s. Archive research turns into detail, form and decoration.

Context
Thesis, First-level Master’s in Accessories Design, Accademia di Costume e Moda, Rome
Role
Archive research, footwear and bag design, technical drawing, layout

Three chapters: the accessory in the 1920s, from the archive to the detail, the collection. Period bags and shoes, cross-stitch motifs, palettes and patterns guide the development.

A collection of pumps, ankle boots and pouch bags, presented through technical drawings, colour variations and the thesis book.

About Agnese Sansonetti

Agnese Sansonetti is a fashion and accessory designer based in Rome. She works across clothing, bags and footwear, from visual research and technical drawing to physical prototypes.
