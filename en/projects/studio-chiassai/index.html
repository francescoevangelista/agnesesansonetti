Studio Chiassai

Internship at a design and styling studio. Work within the team runs from the drawing table to sewn garments, across menswear collections, outerwear and packaging.

Context
Internship, Studio Chiassai Design & Styling, San Giovanni Valdarno (Arezzo). Collaboration with Tatras and projects for other clients
Role
Design team intern: drawing and illustration, menswear flats, collection development, pattern making, sewing and garment making, packaging, graphics

Drawing in the office is the main task: menswear illustrations and technical flats, by hand and digitally. The team develops collections, prints and laser-cut patterns; garments are patterned, cut and sewn in the studio. Graphics in Photoshop and images made with ChatGPT Images support the presentations.

Collection illustrations, finished garments, perforated patterns and the packaging for a 42 × 23.5 cm box, drawn at 1:1 scale.

About Agnese Sansonetti

Agnese Sansonetti is a fashion and accessory designer based in Rome. She works across clothing, bags and footwear, from visual research and technical drawing to physical prototypes.
