Trasparenze Erranti

A collection exploring the tension between the visible and the invisible. Veils, folds and layers shift attention from the gaze to the body, drawing on the theatricality of the 1920s.

Context
Academic project, NABA, Rome
Role
Research, fashion design, collection development

Visual research develops into silhouettes, technical drawings and material studies. The hat conceals the gaze; openings, draping and volumes redefine the relationship between garment and body.

A collection documented through fashion illustrations, technical drawings and a photoshoot. Experimental photography explores doubling, blur and concealment.

About Agnese Sansonetti

Agnese Sansonetti is a fashion and accessory designer based in Rome. She works across clothing, bags and footwear, from visual research and technical drawing to physical prototypes.
