Trasparenze Erranti

A collection exploring the tension between the visible and the invisible, between the theatricality of the 1920s and disorders of sight: cataract, diplopia, scotoma. Veils, folds and layers shift attention from the gaze to the body.

Context
Thesis, First-level Academic Diploma in Fashion Design, NABA, Rome
Role
Research, fashion design, collection development
Credits
Photographer — Simone Panetta
Photo editing — Agnese Sansonetti
Model — Sofia Bettazzi
Stylist — Dania Zaccari
Thesis advisor — Margherita Silvestro

Visual research develops into silhouettes, technical drawings and material studies. The hat conceals the gaze; openings, draping and volumes redefine the relationship between garment and body.

A collection documented through fashion illustrations, technical drawings, a photoshoot, a film and the thesis book. Experimental photography turns doubling, blur and concealment into images.

About Agnese Sansonetti

Agnese Sansonetti is a fashion and accessory designer based in Rome. She works across clothing, bags and footwear, from visual research and technical drawing to physical prototypes.
