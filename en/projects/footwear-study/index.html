Footwear Study

A footwear study connecting construction, contrasting materials and detail. The project is developed through physical prototypes.

Context
Academic project, Accademia di Costume e Moda, Rome
Role
Concept, design, making of the prototypes

From pattern to construction on the last: cutting, assembly, stitching and detail work support the development of the shoe.

Heeled sandal prototypes, made entirely by the designer and documented through overall views and construction details.

About Agnese Sansonetti

Agnese Sansonetti is a fashion and accessory designer based in Rome. She works across clothing, bags and footwear, from visual research and technical drawing to physical prototypes.
