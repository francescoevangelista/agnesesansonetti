Ferragamo

Clean geometry and organic tension. A study of the tote bag interprets the brand’s heritage through construction, handles and colour contrasts.

Context
Academic industry project, Accademia di Costume e Moda
Role
Concept, leather goods, technical drawing, development

The architectural elegance of the 1920s and colour contrasts inspired by Irving Penn guide form and palette. The brand’s identity becomes part of the handle’s structure.

A line of tote bags, colour and construction studies, with documentation of the physical prototype and making process.

About Agnese Sansonetti

Agnese Sansonetti is a fashion and accessory designer based in Rome. She works across clothing, bags and footwear, from visual research and technical drawing to physical prototypes.
