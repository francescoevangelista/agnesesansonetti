Graphic research applied to bags. Photography, illustration and decorative modules become surfaces and colour combinations.

Context

Role
Visual research, print design, graphic application

Three studies explore Man Ray’s images, Geoff McFetridge’s visual language and decorative motifs. Applications to existing brand designs are design exercises.

Graphic proposals for accessories and variation boards. These are not commercial collections by the brands shown.
