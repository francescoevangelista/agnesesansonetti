Santoni

A men’s and women’s capsule developed from weaving, organic forms and craft techniques. The research extends from footwear to leather goods.

Context
Academic industry project, Accademia di Costume e Moda
Role
Concept, footwear, leather goods, technical mockups

Studies of materials, lines and details are followed by sketches, technical drawings and variations. Experiments on shoe lasts test the relationship between straps and volume.

A family of footwear and bags documented through collection boards and physical mockups.

About Agnese Sansonetti

Agnese Sansonetti is a fashion and accessory designer based in Rome. She works across clothing, bags and footwear, from visual research and technical drawing to physical prototypes.
